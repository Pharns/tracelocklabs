# TraceLock Labs

## Passive RF Monitoring for Situational Awareness

TraceLock Labs is an applied research laboratory focused on validated passive multi-protocol RF monitoring under the `TraceLock™` name, evidence-centered telemetry, and future wireless ecosystem Pattern-of-Life (`PoL`) modeling.

## Research Focus

- Passive multi-protocol RF monitoring
- Wireless situational awareness in dense environments
- Evidence-centered telemetry and anomaly detection
- Future wireless ecosystem `PoL` modeling as a baseline-driven analytic direction

## Programs

- SBIR / STTR research programs
- Research partnerships

TraceLock Labs develops the sensing and signal-generation layer within the broader `SDOS` umbrella architecture. `SDOS` is a decision architecture that transforms sensor telemetry into governed, operator-relevant security decisions.
